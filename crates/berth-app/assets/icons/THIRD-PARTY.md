# Third-party icons

Every icon here is a single-colour SVG. It is drawn in the theme's text
colour, or, on an agent's sidebar tile, in white on that brand's own colour
(D53).

## Trademarks

The licences above cover the drawings, not the marks. The Claude, Codex, Copilot,
Mistral, Qwen, Gemini and Cursor marks belong to Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub,
Mistral AI, Alibaba Cloud, Google and Anysphere, and the marks from Simple Icons to
their projects and companies. In Berth they say which program runs in a
session and nothing else.

**They are for dogfooding only.** Before any release: written permission from
each of those companies for its mark, and a review of each other
project's brand policy. Any mark that cannot be cleared is replaced by
Phosphor's generic terminal icon, which is what an unknown program gets
already. This is a release blocker, listed in the macOS runbook beside the
app's own logo.
